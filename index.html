Executive summary

The objective is not to find the most complicated classifier. It is to establish whether a model provides useful ranking, whether its probabilities are informative, and which decisions are justified by the evidence.

The project compares a prior-only DummyClassifier, interpretable LogisticRegression, and a nonlinear RandomForestClassifier. Model comparison and calibration selection occur through stratified cross-validation on the training partition. The operating threshold is selected from out-of-fold training predictions; only then is the chosen workflow assessed on a held-out test set.

Data audit and exploratory analysis

The source dataset describes a fictional provider's customers, with account tenure, billing, service subscriptions, contract type and churn outcome. The row-level target is Churn: Yes is encoded as 1 and No as 0. A customer identifier is not a predictor.

Exploration is used to find data-quality issues and describe associations that motivate questions. Differences in observed churn rates are not causal effects: contract, tenure, service use and customer characteristics can be related to one another.

02

Data quality decisions

Identifier removal

customerID is removed before modeling. It identifies a row, not a generalizable customer behavior pattern. Repeated predictor profiles are retained because different customers can share the same observed values.

TotalCharges parsing

The source stores charges as text. Blank values are inspected alongside tenure. A blank for a zero-tenure customer is set to zero; any other unresolved missing numeric values are imputed within the training pipeline.

Target validation

The expected labels are checked and encoded as 1 for churn and 0 for no churn. Unexpected labels raise an error instead of silently becoming missing targets.

No global-statistic leakage

No dataset-wide median or scaling statistic is fitted before the split. Imputation, scaling and encoding are learned separately inside each training fold and refitted for the final model.

Data-cleaning judgementA generic median replacement for every blank TotalCharges value is not automatically correct. The implemented rule treats zero charges as a domain-based value only when tenure is zero; other missing values remain eligible for train-fitted imputation.
03

Preprocessing as part of the model

The transformation steps are packaged in a scikit-learn Pipeline and ColumnTransformer. This is more than code organization: it enforces the correct fit/transform boundary during cross-validation.

Numeric features

Median imputation for unresolved missing values, followed by standardization. The imputer and scaler learn parameters only from the current training fold.

Categorical features

Most-frequent imputation followed by one-hot encoding. Unknown categories are ignored at transform time instead of causing an inference-time error.

Raw training fold
  ├── numeric pipeline: impute → standardize
  └── categorical pipeline: impute → one-hot encode
Transformed matrix → classifier

Validation fold: transform with fitted training-fold steps only

A model's final performance is still evaluated on rows held aside before model selection. Pipeline correctness prevents leakage in preprocessing but does not by itself prevent every kind of leakage; feature availability and target-derived columns must also be audited.

04

Experimental design

  • Stratified 80/20 split: preserve approximate class prevalence while reserving a final test partition.
  • Five-fold stratified CV on the training partition: compare models using the same folds; report mean and fold-to-fold standard deviation.
  • Model selection: choose among Logistic Regression and Random Forest using mean average precision. The dummy baseline is a reference, not eligible to win.
  • Calibration decision: compare the selected model's raw probabilities with sigmoid calibration using cross-validated Brier score on training data only.
  • Threshold selection: use out-of-fold training probabilities and an explicit minimum-recall target, with higher precision preferred among qualifying thresholds.
  • Final test: report results after model, calibration method and threshold have been selected. Do not tune based on the final test labels.
What the standard deviation meansFold-to-fold variation is useful for seeing whether model quality is stable across training subsets. It is not a 95% confidence interval and does not capture dataset shift, changes over time, or every source of uncertainty.
05

The model candidates

BASELINE

DummyClassifier

Prior-only benchmark

Predicts from the class distribution and provides a floor for comparison. A candidate that cannot beat this baseline in useful ranking or decision metrics has not demonstrated meaningful predictive value.

INTERPRETABLE

Logistic Regression

Regularized linear decision function

Models log-odds as a linear combination of transformed predictors. Its coefficients can support interpretation of conditional associations, subject to encoding, correlated features and model assumptions. They do not establish causal effects.

NONLINEAR

Random Forest

Bagged decision trees

Can capture nonlinear relationships and interactions without manually specifying them. Its probability estimates still require evaluation, and feature importance can be biased or spread across correlated variables.

Gaussian Naive Bayes and an RBF SVM appeared in the original comparison. They are not part of the primary candidate set: Gaussian Naive Bayes' feature-distribution and conditional-independence assumptions are awkward for one-hot categories, while an RBF SVM adds tuning and compute costs without a specific hypothesis that justifies keeping it in this streamlined experiment. They can be added later as training-only candidates if there is a clear research question.

06

Metrics, probability quality and uncertainty

ROC-AUC

Measures ranking discrimination across thresholds. It does not say whether predicted probabilities are calibrated and can look acceptable when the positive-class retrieval performance is weak.

Average precision

Summarizes the precision-recall curve as a stepwise weighted average. Compare it with the positive-class prevalence and do not call it interchangeable with every trapezoidal PR-AUC implementation.

Precision and recall

Precision asks what share of flagged customers churned; recall asks what share of churners were flagged. Increasing recall usually requires contacting more customers, including more false positives.

Brier score and calibration

The mean squared error of predicted probabilities against binary outcomes. Lower is better; the calibration curve compares mean predicted probability with observed event frequency in probability bins.

Bootstrap intervals

Percentile intervals resample held-out test rows and quantify sampling variation conditional on this particular split. They do not account for distribution shift or all sources of uncertainty.

Confusion matrix

True positives are detected churners; false negatives are missed churners; false positives are flagged customers who did not churn; true negatives are correctly unflagged customers.

08

From risk ranking to a retention decision

A risk model can prioritize which customers to investigate, but it cannot infer who will respond to a retention offer. The top-risk analysis measures how many observed churners appear within a constrained contact list. It is not an estimate of customers saved or return on investment.

Risk-capture results will populate after the analysis runs.

Subgroup performance

Recall, precision, observed churn rate and flagged fraction are reported by contract, internet-service, payment-method and tenure segments. Always inspect group size and positive support. A low sample count creates noisy estimates; a segment difference is not, by itself, proof of discrimination or a causal mechanism.

Post-hoc model interpretation

Permutation importance measures the drop in average precision after a feature is shuffled. It indicates reliance by this fitted model on this evaluation sample. Correlated predictors can share or mask importance; this is not a causal effect.

Logistic Regression coefficients and odds ratios

These coefficients come from the tuned Logistic Regression reference candidate fitted on the training partition, whether or not it is selected as the final model. Numeric columns are standardized, so their odds ratios correspond to a one-standard-deviation increase; one-hot coefficients compare a category with its dropped reference level. These are conditional associations, not causal effects.

09

Limitations and next experiments

  • Snapshot, not temporal validation. A later time period would better test deployment generalization and drift.
  • Prediction is not intervention effect. Randomized retention offers or a credible causal design are needed to estimate who can be persuaded to stay.
  • Threshold is illustrative. The 75% recall target must be replaced by a business-approved trade-off informed by contact capacity, offer cost, customer value and expected treatment effect.
  • Uncertainty is conditional. The bootstrap intervals quantify sampling variation on this held-out split but do not fix selection bias in the dataset or guarantee future performance.
  • Correlated features complicate interpretation. Permutation importance is a model diagnostic, not causal attribution.

A credible next study would validate on later-period customers, monitor calibration and ranking over time, and experimentally measure the incremental effect of retention interventions.
